# Kotlin Student Coding Competition — rules snapshot

> Source: [Official Contest Rules](https://kotlinconf.com/contest-rules/)
> Verified: 2 November 2026
> Status: Working summary, not legal advice. Recheck the live page because the sponsor may amend the rules.

## Mandatory entrant gates

The entrant must personally confirm all eligibility facts. Repository inspection cannot establish them.

- At least 18, or the older local age of majority.
- Enrolled full-time in an accredited undergraduate/graduate program when the contest begins, or was enrolled
  full-time within the preceding twelve months.
- Not excluded by the stated sanctions, residency, government-employment, sponsor/member-employment, affiliation,
  or immediate-family restrictions.
- Able to receive the prize under applicable law and policy.

## Contest and entry gates

- Contest period: 16 September 2026 at 09:00 CET through 7 December 2026 at 23:59 CET.
- One entry per person. The submission is individual; team entries are not allowed.
- Build a cross-platform project using Kotlin and upload the code to GitHub.
- The repository must contain an Apache 2.0, MIT, or BSD 3-Clause license.
- Submit the repository link and a 300-word essay through the official form before the deadline.
- The essay covers the entrant's background, relevant experience/hobbies, project idea, code, and technologies.

## Submission gates

- The submission must be created specifically for this contest; a pre-contest existing project is ineligible.
- A bare template or Hello World project is excluded. The result must be substantial and meaningful.
- The submission must be original and owned by the entrant, or the entrant must hold the rights needed to grant
  the license described by the rules. Retain third-party and starter-kit notices and document provenance.
- Include a comprehensive README with reproducible installation and launch steps for every submitted target plus
  instructions for trying the key features.
- A library alone is not an accepted submission; Porta must remain a usable application.
- Statements must not be untruthful, incomplete, inaccurate, or misleading.
- Content and included media must respect third-party IP, privacy/publicity, safety, and the other content
  restrictions in the official rules. Keep written permissions for other identifiable people used in media.

## Judging rubric

- Creativity and novelty of the idea: 40%.
- Use of Kotlin Multiplatform and/or the proportion of Kotlin code shared across platforms: 40%.
- Alignment with the official Kotlin coding conventions: 20%.

Novelty is the tie-break criterion. The rules do not publish a formula for shared-code percentage or a detailed
point breakdown inside each category, so internal scoring must be labeled as an estimate.

## Recommended, not mandatory

- A 3–5 minute screencast demonstrating the main features.
- Joining the contest Slack for announcements and questions.

Do not accidentally report these recommendations as eligibility requirements.
